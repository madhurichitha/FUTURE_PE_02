# FUTURE_PE_02 — AI Content Marketing using UGC Ads

## 1. Project Overview

This project was created as part of the Future Interns Prompt Engineering Internship.

The objective is to create conversion-focused UGC-style advertising content for a real skincare brand using structured AI prompts.

### Brand
Minimalist

### Product
Minimalist SPF 50 Sunscreen

### Campaign Objective
To create authentic, relatable and conversion-focused social media content that increases product awareness, engagement and purchase consideration without using false or unsupported claims.

---

## 2. Target Audience

The primary target audience includes:

- College students and young adults
- Skincare beginners
- People interested in simple and affordable skincare
- People looking for a daily sunscreen

### Common Concerns

- Difficulty choosing simple skincare products
- Need for an easy-to-follow skincare routine
- Interest in daily sun protection
- Preference for relatable and educational content

### Buying Triggers

- Clear product communication
- Simple explanations
- Relatable UGC-style content
- Transparent messaging
- Easy-to-understand calls to action

---

## 3. Brand Tone

The campaign follows a:

- Minimal
- Honest
- Relatable
- Modern
- Educational
- Gen-Z friendly

communication style.

The content avoids forced slang, exaggerated claims and fabricated testimonials.

---

## 4. Content Marketing Angles

Five different content angles were developed:

1. Daily sunscreen awareness
2. Skincare beginner education
3. Simple skincare routine
4. Relatable college/student lifestyle
5. Product consideration and purchase intent

Each angle approaches the same product from a different customer perspective.

---

## 5. UGC Content Strategy

The UGC concepts follow a simple structure:

**Hook → Problem → Relatable Situation → Product Introduction → Product Explanation → CTA**

The scripts are designed for approximately 20–30 second short-form videos.

The scripts are written as potential UGC/ad concepts and do not pretend to represent real customer experiences.

---

## 6. Platform Strategy

### Instagram Reels

Focus:

- Relatable visual storytelling
- Short captions
- Clear CTA

### YouTube Shorts

Focus:
- Immediate problem statement
- Fast-paced educational delivery

### Facebook

Focus:

---

## 8. Conversion-Focused Strategy

The content was designed around the following conversion principles:

- Capture attention quickly
- Identify a relatable customer problem
- Introduce the product naturally
- Explain the product without exaggerated claims
- Use clear and low-pressure CTAs
- Test different hooks and messaging
- Adapt content according to platform behaviour

The objective is to encourage users to learn more and consider the product rather than relying on misleading advertising.

---

## 9. Accuracy & Safety Considerations

The campaign follows these principles:

- No invented clinical results
- No fabricated customer reviews
- No invented discounts or prices
- No unsupported certifications
- No medical treatment or cure claims
- No fabricated personal experiences
- Product-specific claims are kept conservative
- UGC scripts are presented as creative concepts rather than real testimonials

Creative advertising language is clearly distinguished from verified product information.
