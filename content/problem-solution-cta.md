## Campaign Structure

Each advertisement follows:

**Problem → Solution → CTA**

The problem creates relevance, the solution introduces a practical approach, and the CTA encourages the viewer to explore the product without making exaggerated promises.

## Accuracy Note

These are creative advertising concepts. They are not presented as real customer testimonials or personal experiences.

No guaranteed results, medical claims, fabricated reviews, discounts, prices, or unsupported product claims are included.
