# A/B Testing Ideas — Minimalist SPF 50 Sunscreen

## Test 1 — Hook Style

### Version A: Problem Hook
"Still skipping sunscreen because your routine is already too long?"

### Version B: Curiosity Hook
"Your 5-minute morning routine needs one more step."

**Test:** Problem-focused vs curiosity-focused opening.

**Primary metrics:**
- 3-second views
- Average watch time
- Video completion rate

---

## Test 2 — Audience Angle

### Version A: College Student
"College students, this is your sunscreen reminder."

### Version B: Skincare Beginner
"If you're a skincare beginner, start with this step."

**Test:** Lifestyle-specific messaging vs beginner-education messaging.

**Primary metrics:**
- Engagement rate
- Saves
- Shares
- Click-through rate

---

## Test 3 — Video Format

### Version A: Talking-Head UGC
Creator speaks directly to the camera and explains the problem and product.

### Version B: Morning Routine UGC
Creator shows a morning routine with product shots and text overlays.

**Test:** Direct communication vs visual routine storytelling.

**Primary metrics:**
- Watch time
- Completion rate
- Engagement

---

## Test 4 — CTA Style

### Version A: Direct CTA
"Explore Minimalist SPF 50 and check the product details."

### Version B: Soft CTA
"Check the product information and see if it fits your routine."

**Test:** Direct product exploration vs softer decision-oriented CTA.

**Primary metrics:**
- Click-through rate
- Product-page visits
- Engagement

---

## Test 5 — Caption Style

### Version A: Short & Relatable
"POV: You remembered everything except sunscreen 😭☀️"

### Version B: Educational
"Building a skincare routine doesn't have to be complicated. Focus on understanding the basics and staying consistent."

**Test:** Relatable short-form copy vs educational messaging.

**Primary metrics:**
- Saves
- Comments
- Shares
- Engagement rate

---

## Testing Framework

The campaign can be evaluated using:

**Hook → Content Format → Message → CTA → Performance**

The best-performing combination can be identified using platform analytics.

Important metrics include:

- Views
- Watch time
- Completion rate
- Likes
- Comments
- Shares
- Saves
- Click-through rate
- Conversions, where available

A/B tests should change one major variable at a time where possible so that performance differences are easier to interpret.

## Objective

The purpose of A/B testing is to identify which creative elements generate stronger audience attention, engagement and product consideration.
