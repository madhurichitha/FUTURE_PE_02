# Target Audience — Minimalist SPF 50 Sunscreen

## Primary Audience

- College students and young adults
- Skincare beginners
- People interested in simple and affordable skincare
- People looking for a daily sunscreen

## Common Skincare Concerns

- Not knowing where sunscreen fits into a skincare routine
- Wanting a simple daily skincare routine
- Looking for skincare content that is easy to understand
- Difficulty choosing products without complicated explanations
- Preference for relatable content rather than highly technical advertising

## Audience Motivations

The audience is likely to be motivated by:

- Simple and easy-to-understand skincare information
- Convenient daily skincare habits
- Relatable content from creators
- Clear product communication
- Affordable and practical skincare choices

## Buying Triggers

Potential buying triggers include:

- A clear explanation of the product
- Relatable short-form content
- Simple demonstrations of how the product fits into a routine
- Transparent and honest messaging
- A clear call-to-action
- Content that helps the viewer make an informed decision

## Content Strategy

The campaign should communicate the product in a simple, relatable and educational way.

Instead of using exaggerated claims, the content focuses on:

**Awareness → Education → Product Consideration → Action**

All UGC concepts are creative advertising ideas and are not presented as genuine customer testimonials.
