## UGC Content Guidelines

These scripts are creative UGC-style advertising concepts.

They should be presented by a creator as scripted promotional content rather than fabricated customer testimonials.

The scripts avoid:
- Invented customer experiences
- Medical claims
- Guaranteed results
- Unsupported product claims
- Fake reviews

The intended structure is:

**Hook → Problem → Relatable Situation → Product Introduction → Product Explanation → CTA**
