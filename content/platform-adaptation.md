# Platform Adaptation — Minimalist SPF 50 Sunscreen

## Instagram Reels

### Content Style
Fast-paced, visual and relatable UGC.

### Opening
Use a strong hook within the first few seconds.

### Visual Approach
- Morning routine shots
- Product close-ups
- Talking-to-camera clips
- Text overlays
- Quick transitions

### Tone
Casual, relatable and Gen-Z friendly without forced slang.

### CTA
Examples:
- "Save this as your sunscreen reminder."
- "Check out the product details."
- "Explore Minimalist SPF 50."

---

## YouTube Shorts

### Content Style
Short, direct storytelling with an immediate hook.

### Opening
Start directly with a problem, question or relatable situation.

### Visual Approach
- Talking-head delivery
- Quick product shots
- Routine demonstrations
- On-screen text

### Tone
Clear, educational and conversational.

### CTA
Examples:
- "Learn more about the product."
- "Check the product details."
- "Explore this sunscreen option."

---

## Facebook

### Content Style
Slightly more explanatory UGC-style advertising.

### Opening
Start with a relatable skincare problem or everyday situation.

### Visual Approach
- Product demonstration
- Simple routine content
- Short talking-head videos
- Informative text overlays

### Tone
Friendly, informative and trustworthy.

### CTA
Examples:
- "Explore the product."
- "Learn more before you decide."
- "Check the product information."

---

## Cross-Platform Strategy

The core campaign message remains consistent across platforms:

**Relatable Problem → Simple Approach → Product Introduction → CTA**

However, the presentation changes according to the platform.

### Instagram
Prioritize visual engagement and short attention spans.

### YouTube Shorts
Prioritize fast storytelling and immediate information.

### Facebook
Provide slightly more context and explanatory messaging.

## Content Consistency

Across all platforms:

- Maintain the Minimalist brand tone.
- Avoid exaggerated claims.
- Avoid fabricated testimonials.
- Keep UGC concepts natural and relatable.
- Use clear calls to action.
- Present creative scripts as advertising concepts rather than genuine customer experiences.
