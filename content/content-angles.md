# Content Marketing Angles — Minimalist SPF 50 Sunscreen

## Angle 1: Daily Sunscreen Awareness

**Core idea:** Make sunscreen feel like a simple everyday skincare step.

**Message:** Sunscreen can be presented as part of a consistent daily skincare routine.

**Best format:** Morning routine / GRWM Reel

**CTA direction:** Encourage viewers to learn more about the product.

---

## Angle 2: Skincare Beginner

**Core idea:** Speak to people who are overwhelmed by complicated skincare routines.

**Message:** Keep the conversation around daily skincare simple and easy to understand.

**Best format:** Talking-head educational UGC

**CTA direction:** Invite viewers to explore the product.

---

## Angle 3: Simple Skincare Routine

**Core idea:** Show how sunscreen can fit naturally into a minimal routine.

**Message:** Focus on keeping a routine practical and easy to follow.

**Best format:** Quick routine demonstration

**CTA direction:** Encourage viewers to consider adding sunscreen to their routine.

---

## Angle 4: College / Young Adult Lifestyle

**Core idea:** Connect sunscreen with everyday student and young-adult routines.

**Message:** Create relatable situations such as getting ready for college, going outdoors, or heading out with friends.

**Best format:** POV / lifestyle UGC Reel

**CTA direction:** Encourage viewers to learn more.

---

## Angle 5: Product Consideration

**Core idea:** Help viewers evaluate whether the product fits their skincare needs.

**Message:** Present the product clearly without exaggerated promises or fabricated experiences.

**Best format:** Product-focused UGC / review-style concept

**CTA direction:** Encourage viewers to check the product details before purchasing.

---

## Overall Campaign Approach

The five angles move the audience through:

**Awareness → Relatability → Education → Product Consideration → Action**

All concepts are creative advertising ideas and should not be presented as genuine customer testimonials unless based on a real, verifiable experience.
